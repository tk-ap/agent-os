# ALVIRA Ecosystem Contract

Version: 0.1.0

The ecosystem turns context and portfolio signals into governed work, uses the best available agentic harness for execution, and learns from measured outcomes.

## Product boundaries

### ALVIRA - context intelligence

Creates, validates, versions, and maintains durable human and organizational context. ALVIRA is the source of truth for context; it does not own destination-specific distribution, workforce coordination, portfolio prioritization, or runtime authorization.

### ALVIRA Bridge - context interoperability

Distributes approved ALVIRA context to connected tools and agents. Bridge governs what context a consumer may know. It does not create a competing context store or grant operational authority.

### Ailhat - portfolio intelligence

Observes products and portfolios, detects what deserves attention, prioritizes initiatives, and measures outcomes. Ailhat may initiate work but does not own canonical agents, skills, routing, runtime authority, or harness-specific execution.

### Agent OS / Workflow Studio - workforce intelligence

Organizes agents, skills, teams, workflows, routing, and handoffs around an accepted objective. It chooses the minimum sufficient workforce and an appropriate execution harness, but does not invent portfolio priority or grant itself authority.

### Ledgato - authorization intelligence

Maps, verifies, approves, denies, enforces, and proves what agents may do. Ledgato governs operational action, not context authorship, portfolio priority, or workforce planning.

### Agentic Harness Layer - leverage and execution

Performs work through the best available agentic environment. cto.new, Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, custom agents, APIs, and automations are examples. Harnesses are replaceable execution substrates, not owners of portfolio decisions or business authority.

## Core distinction

Bridge governs what agents may know. Ledgato governs what agents may do.

## Default loop

1. ALVIRA maintains relevant context.
2. Ailhat detects and prioritizes an opportunity, risk, drift, or required work.
3. Agent OS composes the minimum sufficient workforce and execution plan.
4. Bridge supplies only the context authorized for that initiative and workforce.
5. Ledgato resolves effective authority and returns allow, deny, or approval-required decisions.
6. An appropriate agentic harness executes bounded work.
7. Execution returns evidence and an outcome event.
8. Ailhat measures the result; Steward reviews whether to keep, accelerate, change, or stop.

## Operating rules

- Every product must remain independently valuable.
- Shared contracts connect products; duplicated capability does not.
- Product agents must protect their declared boundaries.
- Cross-product work is proposed to the owning product through a contract, not silently implemented in the wrong repository.
- Cross-marketing must follow a completed value moment and solve the user's next visible problem.
- Harness-specific assumptions stay inside adapters.
- Context and authority are least-privilege by default.
- Execution is incomplete until evidence returns and the intended outcome is measured.
