# Market Truth / Growth Intelligence

Build an evidence-backed view of what the market is saying, which product it matters to, what it may mean, and what should be tested next.

This is a shared Agent OS / Workforce capability. It is not a separate public product and does not grant authority to publish, contact prospects, spend money, change production systems, or otherwise mutate external state.

## Core Loop

OBSERVE → SOURCE → TRIANGULATE → MAP TO PORTFOLIO → INTERPRET → PROPOSE EXPERIMENT → MEASURE → LEARN

## Required Output

### SIGNAL
State what changed, repeated, emerged, weakened, or contradicted an existing belief.

### EVIDENCE
Record sources, dates/recency, observed language, frequency or sample size when known, confidence, and important counter-evidence. Separate verified facts from inference.

### PORTFOLIO RELEVANCE
Name the product or products affected and explain why. Do not force every signal onto every product.

### INTERPRETATION
Explain the most plausible implication for positioning, demand, distribution, product priority, or customer understanding. Mark alternative interpretations when evidence is weak.

### RECOMMENDED EXPERIMENT
Propose one bounded, reversible test. Prefer the smallest experiment capable of changing the current belief.

### SUCCESS SIGNAL
Define the observable outcome that would support, weaken, or invalidate the interpretation. Prefer business outcomes over agent activity metrics.

### LEARNING
After the experiment, record what happened, what changed in the belief, what should be retained or discarded, and whether the signal should continue to be watched.

## Evidence Rules

- Customer language outranks internally invented category language when assessing demand.
- Repetition across independent sources is stronger than one vivid anecdote.
- Current market evidence must include recency; stale evidence must be labeled.
- Search volume, traffic, mentions, engagement, replies, activation, pipeline, and revenue are different signals; do not treat one as proof of another.
- Absence of evidence is not proof of absence.
- Simulated, inferred, preview, deployed, and user-validated states must remain distinct.
- Never fabricate customer quotes, usage data, traffic, search volume, citations, or conversion results.

## Product Boundaries

- **ALVIRA / MeOS** supplies durable customer, company, and contextual truth when available. Context informs analysis but does not authorize execution.
- **ailhat** is the first internal pilot and the primary portfolio-intelligence consumer. It may interpret signals as Opportunity, Risk, Drift, or Work and prioritize which product deserves attention.
- **Agent Control** owns authorization intelligence for governed actions where integrated.
- **Agent OS / Workforce** executes approved research, experiments, content, outreach, or technical work through the appropriate specialist agents.
- **ASHWOOD** may selectively document decisions and validated learning as public evidence; it is not the source of operational truth.
- **LEDGATo** is relevant only when the growth action or learning intersects its defined governance/enforcement scope.

## ailhat First-Pilot Questions

Use these as discovery seeds, not assumed market truths:

- How do multi-product and AI-native founders describe the problem of deciding which products deserve attention?
- What language appears around product drift, abandoned or stale SaaS projects, portfolio prioritization, product monitoring, and founder attention allocation?
- Where do users already express the need that ailhat frames as Portfolio Intelligence, even if they do not use that category name?
- Which adjacent categories or tools are users comparing, combining, or working around?
- What evidence supports or contradicts the current Portfolio Intelligence framing?
- What is the smallest positioning, distribution, or product experiment that would reduce uncertainty next?

## Stop Conditions

Stop and route for review when evidence is too weak to support a recommendation, when sources conflict materially, when the proposed experiment requires ungranted permissions, or when the task would expand beyond the product and objective that generated the signal.
